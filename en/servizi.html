01

Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy addresses the prevention, assessment and treatment of musculoskeletal dysfunctions caused by injuries, surgery or overuse conditions. Through hands-on techniques, therapeutic exercise and targeted recovery protocols based on objective measures, we work to restore proper functional balance.

Every pathway is personalised to the patient’s condition and goals: the aim is to regain strength, mobility and confidence in movement, while reducing the risk of re-injury. We also provide neurological, geriatric and cardio-respiratory physiotherapy pathways.

Our areas of focus

  • → Orthopaedic and musculoskeletal rehabilitation
  • → Post-operative rehabilitation
  • → Shoulder, knee, hip and cervical spine
  • → Chronic and rheumatological pain
  • → Temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders

02

Sports physiotherapy

Whether you are a professional athlete or a recreational sportsperson, we support you from injury management right through to your return to sport. Our team has worked with football, rugby and basketball teams, ATP tournaments and clinics specialising in sports trauma.

The pathway includes a dedicated return to sport phase, built on functional tests and scientifically validated outcome measures. These allow us to track progress objectively and ensure a safe, effective and lasting return to sport.

Ideal for

  • → Muscle, ligament and tendon injuries
  • → Rehabilitation after ACL reconstruction
  • → Running and overuse injuries
  • → Strength testing and return-to-sport criteria

03

Home-visit physiotherapy

Home-visit physiotherapy is designed for those who find it difficult to reach the clinic but still want treatment of the same quality and professionalism. The physiotherapist travels directly to the patient’s home, bringing the equipment needed to deliver sessions safely and effectively.

This service ensures continuity of care, which is essential especially after surgery or in cases of chronic conditions, in a familiar and comfortable environment.

Ideal for

  • → The first weeks after surgery
  • → People with reduced mobility
  • → Older patients or those with chronic conditions

04

Prevention and Strength & Conditioning

The Strength & Conditioning programme combines strength, endurance and motor control exercises to improve physical performance and prevent injuries. It is suitable both for athletes and for anyone wishing to regain or maintain an efficient level of fitness after a period of inactivity or a rehabilitation pathway.

Through personalised, progressive protocols we work on power, stability, coordination and mobility, reducing the risk of re-injury and preventing muscle imbalances or overloading.

Ideal for

  • → End of a rehabilitation pathway
  • → Athletic preparation and injury prevention
  • → Returning to activity after a break
→ Book
05

Massage and lymphatic drainage

Therapeutic massage helps to reduce muscle tension and stiffness, supports recovery after sporting activity and restores a sense of wellbeing.

Manual lymphatic drainage is a gentle, rhythmic technique that promotes the healthy flow of lymph around the body, reducing swelling, oedema and fluid retention. It is particularly indicated after surgery, trauma or in cases of lymphatic insufficiency, and has a relaxing and restorative effect on the whole body.

Ideal for

  • → Swelling and oedema after surgery or trauma
  • → Lymphatic insufficiency
  • → Muscle tension and sports recovery

→ What our studio offers

The tools we use every day, explained briefly. Always combined with exercise and manual therapy.

01

Tecar

Tecar therapy uses radiofrequency electromagnetic energy that stimulates tissues from within, improving circulation and reducing pain and inflammation. We combine it with manual therapy and exercise, especially in the phases where pain limits movement.

02

Shockwave therapy

Shockwaves are high-energy acoustic pulses that stimulate tissue repair and neoangiogenesis. They are useful in treating bone marrow oedema and certain chronic tendon conditions (such as plantar fasciitis and calcific tendinopathy of the shoulder), effectively reducing pain and speeding up healing.

03

BFR

Blood Flow Restriction Therapy involves performing exercises with a partial restriction of blood flow, achieved with a pressure-controlled cuff. It promotes muscle growth and strength even with reduced loads, while reducing joint stress: ideal in the early stages of rehabilitation.

04

Motion Guidance

A visual feedback system: a small laser pointer attached to the body projects the movement onto a target, so the patient can see and correct the quality of their movement in real time. We use it to train motor control, stability and proprioception, for example of the neck, knee and ankle.

05

Dynamometer testing

With the dynamometer we measure muscle strength objectively. We compare the injured side with the healthy one, monitor progress session by session, and use this data as criteria for returning to sport.

06

Cryo-compression

Cryo-compression combines cold with intermittent pneumatic compression to reduce pain, swelling and oedema, particularly in the early stages after surgery or an acute injury.

07

NMES

Neuromuscular electrical stimulation (NMES) uses electrical impulses to trigger muscle contraction, helping to restore strength and tone after immobilisation. It is particularly useful when, following surgery or in the presence of pain, the body limits muscle activation (arthrogenic muscle inhibition).

08

Taping and bracing

Neuromuscular taping and functional bracing stabilise and support joints and muscles without limiting their movement. Very popular in sport, they are also helpful in post-injury recovery. We also help you choose suitable bracing and supports, from injury through to return to sport.
