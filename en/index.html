→ The Hub

FisioHub was born from the idea of three colleagues united by a passion for physiotherapy and a shared vision: to put the real well-being of the person at the centre. The name “Hub” represents a point of connection, the place where science meets experience, where method and listening come together to build effective and personalised treatment pathways. We believe in physiotherapy grounded in scientific evidence, but deeply attentive to the uniqueness of every individual.

Every patient is guided step by step towards their goals, to regain balance, movement and a healthier lifestyle.

Our symbol, a minimal and essential “H”, is meant to represent exactly this: a central hub, the place where pathways, expertise and people come together to deliver a complete, continuous and mindful recovery.

Our approach aims to be contemporary, trustworthy, movement-oriented and far removed from the clichés of traditional physiotherapy. That is why we also embrace technology, with state-of-the-art machines and tools that make our physiotherapy modern and effective.

→ What’s in the studio

State-of-the-art technology for an advanced rehabilitation journey.

01

Tecar

Radiofrequency energy to help reduce pain and inflammation.

02

Shockwave therapy

Extracorporeal shockwave therapy (ESWT) to support neovascularisation and tissue regeneration.

03

BFR

Blood Flow Restriction: strength training even with light loads.

04

Motion Guidance

Visual feedback to see and correct movement.

05

Dynamometer testing

Strength measured in numbers, not by eye.

06

Cryo-compression

Cold and compression against pain and swelling.

07

NMES

Neuromuscular electrical stimulation to activate muscles, prevent muscle wasting and rebuild muscle strength.

08

Taping and bracing

Support for joints and muscles, both on and off the pitch.
