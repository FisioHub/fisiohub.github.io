→ The team

Physiotherapists with advanced university training who live sport first-hand.

→ Luca Gilardoni

  • OMPT
  • CAS SUPSI

Areas of focus: Shoulder · Cervical spine · TMJ

Luca, born in Sorengo in 1991 and raised in Valsolda on the shores of Lake Lugano, has always been passionate about sport. After around twenty years of football, a few injuries drew him towards the world of physiotherapy. A lover of the mountains, he skied for many years and from 2015 devoted himself to mountain running, racing from 2016 with ASD Atletica Pidaggia 1528 and standing out in long distances on the Italian and international Skyrunning circuit.

He graduated in physiotherapy from the University of Insubria and subsequently obtained the OMPT title through the master’s degree in rehabilitation of musculoskeletal disorders at the University of Genoa, an experience that gave him a highly evidence-based approach to both his work and his training.

Read the full biography

He then continued his postgraduate university education by completing the CAS in Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy at SUPSI University in Lugano.

He regularly attends continuing education courses in musculoskeletal and sports medicine, and has collaborated on various research studies with related publications.

Luca began his career at the Istituto Ortopedico Gaetano Pini in Milan, working in orthopaedic and neurosurgical rehabilitation. From 2015 he has worked in Ticino at a practice specialising in musculoskeletal rehabilitation, where he discovered the chance to combine his two passions: sport and physiotherapy.

From 2019 to 2022 he looked after the rehabilitation of the players of FC Gambarogno Contone, a club in the Swiss 2nd Liga Interregional. He then broadened his experience in the sports sector, deepening his specialisation in the treatment of the shoulder, knee and cervical region.

In his free time he spends time with his daughter Viola, enjoys reading, cheering on Como 1907 football club, spending time in nature to take in the landscapes, and travelling!

→ Simone Vaccani

  • Maitland
  • Running Injury Specialist
  • Certificate in Sports Physiotherapy

Areas of focus: Hip · Knee · Foot and ankle

Simone, born in Busto Arsizio (VA) in 1981, has been passionate about sport since he was young.

After amateur football, at the age of 18 he discovered swimming, which for over ten years became his main commitment, leading him to gain instructor qualifications and to compete on the master swimming circuit, both in the pool and in open water.

Read the full biography

He still practises several sports at amateur level, including open water swimming, running, mountain biking and snowboarding, and in his free time he loves hiking in the mountains with his dogs and his family.

Simone graduated in physiotherapy from the University of Varese with top marks, and immediately began his training in manual therapy according to the Maitland Concept, working alongside one of the most experienced lecturers in the method.

Over the years he developed a solid expertise in orthopaedic and sports medicine, earning the international Running Injury Specialist certification from The Running Clinic, a master’s in sports physiotherapy and various courses on musculoskeletal problems of the hip and knee.

In 2019 he moved to Ortisei, in South Tyrol, to work at Dolomiti Sportclinic, a centre of reference for sports trauma and winter sports. During the three years he spent there, he worked alongside surgeons in the operating theatre and followed the recovery of young athletes and high-level professionals on a daily basis, including hockey players, freestyle skiers, biathletes and figure skaters.

From 2022 Simone has been living in Switzerland, where he continues to work in orthopaedic and sports rehabilitation, refining his skills in particular in the rehabilitation of the hip and knee joints.

→ Carola Isella

  • CSCS
  • Maitland
  • Master’s in Lymphatic Drainage
  • Pilates Reformer

Areas of focus: Sports physiotherapy · Strength & Conditioning · Lymphatic drainage · Chronic pain and scoliosis

Carola was born in Biella (BI) in 1996. She has always been an active person, with a strong passion for sport and nature. A former classical ballet dancer, she developed a deep interest in human movement and in recovery from injury, which led her to graduate in physiotherapy from the University of Insubria in Varese.

After completing her studies, Carola deepened her training in the musculoskeletal field by following the Maitland pathway. She has also attended numerous sports physiotherapy courses, earned the CSCS (Certified Strength and Conditioning Specialist) certification and obtained a Master’s in lymphatic drainage. Over the course of her career she has further broadened her expertise, gaining specific qualifications in the treatment of scoliosis and chronic pain.

Read the full biography

Today Carola is on the other side of the lectern: she is a teaching assistant at LUDES University in Lugano.

Carola began working straight away in musculoskeletal and sports rehabilitation, going on to become the lead physiotherapist for high-level rugby and basketball teams.

Carola has gained wide experience across many sports, including rugby, basketball, volleyball and tennis, covering ATP tournaments and working in highly specialised clinics based on scientific evidence. Over the course of her career she has also become a certified Pilates Reformer instructor.

Carola’s approach is grounded in evidence-based practice, with a strong focus on guiding the patient towards a complete recovery. Great importance is placed on patient education, helping them understand their injury and manage it effectively in the long term.

→ Federico Monzoni

  • Master’s in Clinical and Sports Biomechanics

Areas of focus: Biomechanical analysis and return-to-play training · Running specialist · Lumbar spine

Federico, born in the Marche region in 1999, has always been passionate about sport and outdoor activities. Holidays spent in the Sibillini Mountains led him, from childhood, to develop a strong bond with the mountains and to take up mountain biking.

He began downhill racing at a young age and, during his competitive career, took part in national and international competitions, winning two Italian titles in the youth categories and a Swiss title on the IXS Swiss Cup circuit. In 2016 he represented Italy at the Downhill World Championships in Val di Sole.

Read the full biography

Once his competitive career was over, he pursued a degree in physiotherapy at the University of Perugia, graduating with top marks in November 2021. During his studies he won an Erasmus+ scholarship, which allowed him to complete a clinical placement at the orthopaedic clinic of the University of Malaga.

He began his clinical practice in Central Italy, looking after patients with musculoskeletal and neurological conditions, both in the acute and chronic phases.

He then continued his training through further courses in Italy, Switzerland and England, focusing in particular on musculoskeletal and sports medicine.

In 2025 he obtained the Master’s in Clinical and Sports Biomechanics at the University of Camerino, strengthening his interest in movement analysis and in the relationship between biomechanics, rehabilitation and performance.

Early in 2026 he also published, as co-author, his first scientific article on the study of the lower limb, bringing a research dimension to his clinical practice.

In his free time he enjoys travelling, outdoor sports and working on projects linked to the development of sport in the local area.

→ Our credentials, explained

What the titles next to our names mean.

OMPT

Orthopaedic Manipulative Physical Therapist

Internationally recognised title awarded by the IFOMPT, obtained through a university master’s degree in rehabilitation of musculoskeletal disorders. It indicates advanced training in manual therapy, therapeutic exercise and clinical reasoning.

CAS

Certificate of Advanced Studies

Postgraduate university qualification from the Swiss system. Luca has obtained the CAS in Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy at SUPSI.

CSCS

Certified Strength and Conditioning Specialist

International certification from the NSCA (National Strength and Conditioning Association) for designing strength and conditioning training, from injury prevention to performance.

Maitland

Maitland Concept of manual therapy

A training pathway in manual therapy that combines mobilisation techniques with structured clinical reasoning and continuous reassessment of results.

Running Injury Specialist

The Running Clinic

International certification dedicated to the prevention and treatment of running injuries.

Master

Postgraduate university master’s degree

Advanced university training after a physiotherapy degree: in our team, in lymphatic drainage and in clinical and sports biomechanics.
