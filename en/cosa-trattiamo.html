→ What we treat

From the neck to the ankle, from chronic pain to sports injuries: here’s how we can help you.

01

Neck and back

Neck and back pain are among the most common reasons people come to see a physiotherapist. In most cases they are not linked to serious damage, but they can greatly limit your work, your sleep and your sport.

After a thorough assessment, we combine manual therapy, education and a progressive exercise programme to reduce your pain and help you regain confidence in movement.

For example, we treat

  • → Acute or chronic neck pain and low back pain
  • → Disc herniation and bulging disc
  • → Sciatica and pain radiating to the arm or leg
  • → Whiplash
  • → Rehabilitation after spinal surgery
02

Shoulder

Pain when you move your arm or during sport? The shoulder is a very mobile joint and, for that reason, vulnerable to overload, injury and stiffness.

We work on mobility, strength and control with a gradual programme that gets you back to everyday and sporting movements.

For example, we treat

  • → Rotator cuff tears and tendinopathy
  • → Frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis)
  • → Instability and dislocation
  • → Rehabilitation after shoulder surgery
03

Knee

The knee is one of the joints most stressed in sport and in everyday life. We treat acute injuries as well as overuse pain and degenerative problems.

With targeted exercises, objective strength measurements and functional tests, we follow every stage, from restoring mobility to returning to running and sport.

For example, we treat

  • → Anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury
  • → Meniscal injuries
  • → Patellofemoral pain
  • → Patellar tendinopathy
  • → Osteoarthritis and knee replacement
04

Ankle and foot

An ankle sprain that is not treated properly can become a recurring problem. Similarly, heel or Achilles tendon pain can stop you from walking and running comfortably.

We work with advanced technology, along with exercises for strength, balance and proprioception, to reduce pain, restore stability and lower the risk of further injury.

For example, we treat

  • → Ankle sprain and ankle instability
  • → Plantar fasciitis
  • → Achilles tendinopathy
  • → Recovery after fractures and immobilisation

Post-operative rehabilitation

Have you had surgery?

Hip or knee replacement, ACL reconstruction, rotator cuff, spine: we support you through every phase, from managing pain and swelling to getting back to your activities. With cryo-compression, NMES and BFR from the first weeks, and home visits if needed.

05

Hip and groin

Hip and groin pain is common in athletes who run, kick and change direction, but also in people who have osteoarthritis or have had surgery.

A precise assessment helps us understand where the pain is coming from and build a progressive loading programme for the hip, the adductor muscles and the glutes.

For example, we treat

  • → Athletic groin pain
  • → Femoroacetabular impingement (FAI)
  • → Gluteal tendinopathy
  • → Osteoarthritis and hip replacement
06

Jaw (TMJ)

The temporomandibular joint (TMJ) connects the lower jaw to the skull. Jaw pain, clicking, difficulty opening your mouth, bruxism or headaches can all be linked to a problem with this joint.

We treat it with manual therapy, specific exercises and education and, where necessary, work alongside your dentist or orthodontist.

For example, we treat

  • → Pain in the jaw and chewing muscles
  • → Clicking or joint locking
  • → Limited mouth opening
  • → Bruxism and associated headache
07

Chronic pain

Rheumatic conditions and chronic pain need a gentle, ongoing approach, aimed at improving quality of life and reducing disability.

Treatment combines controlled movement, manual therapy and education about pain and lifestyle. The aim is to help you manage your pain and regain balance and independence in your everyday activities.

For example, we treat

  • → Fibromyalgia
  • → Chronic neck pain and low back pain
  • → Chronic joint pain and osteoarthritis
  • → Rheumatoid arthritis
08

Sports injuries

We are sportspeople ourselves, so we know how important it is to get back on the pitch, the track or the trail in the shortest possible time, but without taking risks.

Every programme ends with a return-to-sport phase based on functional tests and objective strength measurements, so that we can decide, using real data, when you are ready.

For example, we treat

  • → Muscle injuries (strains and tears)
  • → Sprains and ligament injuries
  • → Overuse tendinopathies
  • → Running injuries
